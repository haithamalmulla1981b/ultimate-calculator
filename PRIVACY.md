# Privacy Policy — Ultimate Calculator

_Last updated: 7 November 2026_

Ultimate Calculator ("the app") is developed by H.M.Al-Mulla.

**The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal information.**

- **Calculations, history and settings** are saved only on your device. You can delete
  history at any time in the app (History → Clear history, or Settings → Delete all history).
  Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.
- **No account** is required and no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries are included.
- **Internet use:** the app connects to the internet only when you tap **Update rates** in the
  currency converter. It then downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API
  (https://open.er-api.com). No personal data or calculations are sent; as with any web request,
  that service receives your device's IP address. The downloaded rates are stored on your device.
- **Permissions:** the app requests only the Internet permission, used solely for the currency
  converter.
- **Children:** the app is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly collect any data
  from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be raised at
